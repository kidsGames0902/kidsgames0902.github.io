Fix the Wrong World – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Fix the Wrong World is a puzzle game for children and families, made by Mounika. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or any other personal information, and it does not use any account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins) and sound settings are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not send any information to us or to any third party. It works fully offline. It contains no in-app purchases. The only link in the game is to this privacy policy (in Settings), which opens in your web browser.

Children

The game is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country).

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data (for example, ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
