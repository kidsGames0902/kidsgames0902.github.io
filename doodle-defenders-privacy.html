Doodle Defenders – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Doodle Defenders is a casual drawing game made by Mounika. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or any other personal information, and it does not use any account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

Your best score, whether you have finished the tutorial, and your sound setting are saved only on your own device. This information never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not send any information to us or to any third party. It works fully offline and contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that use data (for example, ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
