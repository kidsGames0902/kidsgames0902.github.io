Privacy Policy – Memory Adventure

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Memory Adventure ("the game") is made by Mounika. This page explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (stars and settings) is stored only on your own device.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. Please see Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area and the UK, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • On iPhone and iPad, you will be asked for permission before your advertising ID is used for tracking.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
