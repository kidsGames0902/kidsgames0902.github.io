Privacy Policy – Memory Adventure

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Memory Adventure ("the game") is made by Mounika. The game is designed for families and is suitable for children. We take children's privacy seriously. This page explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photos, location, or contacts, has no accounts or chat, and we do not run our own servers. Game progress (stars and sound settings) is stored only on the player's own device and never leaves it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Because children may play the game, every ad request is marked as child-directed (under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). This means:

  • Only non-personalized ads are shown. No interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • Only ads rated suitable for general audiences ("G") are shown.
  • The advertising ID is not used to track players or build profiles.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process limited technical information such as the device type, operating system, IP address (to estimate an approximate country), and how ads are shown and tapped, as permitted for child-directed services. Google AdMob takes part in the Google Play Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. Learn more: Google's child-directed treatment and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads are shown only when a level is finished or when the player chooses to watch one to get a hint or extra time.

Parents

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data for us to review or delete. Progress can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. If you have any questions or concerns about your child's privacy, please contact us at the email address below and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
